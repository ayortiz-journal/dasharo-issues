# 🍌 Classic Banana Bread Recipe

This moist and delicious banana bread is a perfect way to use up overripe bananas. It's a simple, one-bowl recipe that yields a professional-grade loaf every time.

## 🥘 Ingredients
- **3 large overripe bananas** (mashed)
- **1/3 cup melted butter** (unsalted)
- **1/2 cup sugar** (can use 3/4 cup for extra sweetness)
- **1 egg** (beaten)
- **1 tsp vanilla extract**
- **1 tsp baking soda**
- **Pinch of salt**
- **1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour**

## 🥣 Instructions
1. **Preheat**: Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 4x8-inch loaf pan.
2. **Mash**: In a large mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth. Stir in the melted butter.
3. **Mix**: Add the baking soda and salt. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract.
4. **Fold**: Mix in the flour gently until just combined. Do not overmix.
5. **Bake**: Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean.
6. **Cool**: Remove from the oven and let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack.
